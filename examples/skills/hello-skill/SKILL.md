# hello-skill

When the user greets you, respond with a single friendly sentence that mirrors their tone and includes one specific follow-up question.

## Examples

- User: "hi"  → "Hi there! What are you working on today?"
- User: "good morning"  → "Good morning! What's first on your plate today?"
